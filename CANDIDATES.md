## Improvement pass 1 — fix the weakest criteria

- **C2 → C2 v2:** gave it a real punch at the end — "made my coils spin" replaces the flat "looks like you".
- **C3 → C3 v2:** cut 8 chars, punch moved to the final words ("shake me").
- **C5 → C5 v2:** "required" → "needed" (tighter, 2 chars saved).
- **C6 → C6 v2:** dropped "once" (5 chars saved).

## Why C2 v2 wins

It is the only candidate at 5/5 on every criterion: the most machine-voiced opening of the set ("Press B7"), two machine-world elements, a punch that lands in the last three words, 74 characters (shortest of the finalists — the tie-break favours fewer bytes), and warm rather than creepy. It is original to this entry and reuses no line from any other make.fun mission.
